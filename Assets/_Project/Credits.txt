Third-party assets used in this project
=======================================

Everything else (code, level, materials, UI) was made for this project.
All of the assets below are CC0 (public domain), so credit isn't required,
but I'm listing them anyway so it's clear where they came from.


Characters - "Animated Characters 2" by Kenney
  https://opengameart.org/content/animated-characters-2
  https://www.kenney.nl
  - Used: characterMedium.fbx, the idle / run / jump animations, and 3 of the 4 skins
    (skaterMaleA for the player, criminalMaleA for patrollers, cyborgFemaleA for chasers)
  - Enemies are the same model scaled down to fit their colliders
  - Original license file: Art/Characters/License.txt


Sound effects - "The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection [512 sounds]" by Juhani Junkala
  https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style
  - Picked 7 out of the 512:
      jump            sfx_movement_jump1
      coin            sfx_coin_single2
      stomp           sfx_sounds_impact2
      hurt            sfx_sounds_damage1
      death           sfx_sounds_negative2
      checkpoint      sfx_sounds_powerup3
      level complete  sfx_sounds_fanfare1
  - Original info/license file: Audio/INFO.txt
